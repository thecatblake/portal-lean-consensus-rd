# Lean Consensus R&D

A public research log on Lean Consensus, the next-generation Ethereum consensus layer. Notes and findings are published when they are ready.

Primary readers are consensus client implementers and protocol researchers. Some articles are written for the broader Ethereum community.

Requirements and decisions: [docs/REQUIREMENTS.md](docs/REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Related sites

- [leanroadmap.org](https://leanroadmap.org/) is a different website: a progress dashboard for Lean Consensus R&D. This site is separate from it and is not affiliated with it.

## Publishing

1. Write or edit a page or article in Portal and publish it.
2. Trigger a Cloudflare Pages build (a push to the repository, or a manual redeploy). The build fetches the new snapshot.

## Editorial rules

- Every article has one type, Research or Explainer, stored as a keyword.
- Every article shows its author name.
- Every factual claim about a spec, PR, commit or paper links to the exact primary source. Opinions and speculation are labeled as such. Numbers are measured, never estimated.

The type label and byline display are decided but not built yet. See the requirements doc.
